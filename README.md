## 🚧 The Problem: The "Silent Wall" in Higher Education

Prelingual Deaf students face a deeper challenge than just hearing loss—it’s a **structural language barrier** embedded in modern education systems.

### 🔹 The Literacy Gap

- Sign Language is often the **first language (L1)**
- Written English is a **second language (L2)** with different syntax
- Reading academic PDFs becomes cognitively exhausting

### 🔹 The Visibility Problem

- Critical updates (CATs, exams) are hidden in:
  - LMS systems
  - Email threads
- Students often discover them **too late**

### 🔹 Interpreter Scarcity

- Severe shortage of **STEM-specialized interpreters**
- No real-time support during technical lectures

### 🔹 Cognitive Overload

- Constant switching between:
  - Notes
  - Dictionaries
  - Videos
- Leads to **mental fatigue instead of learning**

---

## 💡 The Solution

SENSI shifts the burden of accessibility from the student to an **autonomous AI agent**.

### ⚡ Core Functionality

- Converts:
  - 📄 Text (PDFs, notes, LMS content)
  - 🎤 Speech (lectures)
- Into:
  - ✋ Real-time **ASL stickman animations**

---

## 🎯 Why Stickman Animation?

### 📶 Low-Bandwidth Friendly

- Works on limited internet access
- No heavy video streaming required

### 👁️ Clarity Over Realism

- Avoids uncanny valley
- Focuses on:
  - Hand shapes
  - Motion clarity

### ⚡ Real-Time Performance

- Instant rendering
- No lag in live translation

## 🚀 Features

- 📄 **PDF Overlay Assistant**  
  Highlight text → instant ASL translation

- 🎥 **Live Lecture Bridge (Planned)**  
  Real-time speech + gesture translation

- 📚 **Gloss Library**  
  Community-driven dictionary for technical signs

- 🤖 **Autonomous Agent**  
  Detects and surfaces:
  - Deadlines
  - Announcements
  - Academic updates
